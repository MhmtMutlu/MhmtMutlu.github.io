## What it is

FORMA guides you through gym workouts: animated exercise demonstrations, set and rest tracking, an AI coach, connections with trainers and a weekly league to keep things competitive.

The iPhone app is available through TestFlight; Android is coming soon. The public repository hosts the landing page.
